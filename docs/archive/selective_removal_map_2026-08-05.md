## Manual Review / Confidence Notes

- The backend and Web UI are mixed files, so neither may be replaced or deleted.
- Generic ROS/JAKA clients in `DualJakaWebNode.__init__` are preserved when shared
  by basic controls. Servo-specific clients are not exposed after route removal;
  they may remain inactive if removing them risks shared transport behavior.
- `dual_arm_app/config/world_frame_calibration.json`, object files, exports,
  experiment logs, and historical `*.bak*` files are not deleted in this task.
- Custom ROS packages are outside the active FastAPI/Web selective-removal surface
  and contain mixed research functions. They are preserved pending manual review.
